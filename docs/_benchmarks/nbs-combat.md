## Description
The Combat of Retrofitting Urban Drainage Networks with Nature-Based Solutions is a benchmark focused on integrating nature-based solutions into an existing urban drainage system. The benchmark includes a calibrated SWMM model, rainfall and temperature data, implementation constraints, and evaluation metrics used to compare different retrofit strategies. It was developed as part of the UDM 2025 challenge and serves as a benchmark for research on sustainable urban drainage and stormwater management.
